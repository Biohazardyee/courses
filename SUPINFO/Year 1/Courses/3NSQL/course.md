# 3NSQL

## Course Objective 

- Undestand the fundamentals of NoSQL DB
- Understand the advantages and limitations of NoSQL DB compared to SQL DB
- Explore different types of NoSQL databases (Document, Key-Value, Column-Family, Graph)
- Learn to design and modelize NoSQL databases
- Acquire skills in querying and manipulating NoSQL databases

## The Fundamentals 

NoSQL = Not Only SQL

- Database management systems for databases, created to answer specific needs historically treated by the SGBDR (Relational Database Management Systems)
- Modern stack services and Big Data
